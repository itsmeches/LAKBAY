# CHED policy clarification register

Source: issued CMO No. 24, s. 2026, 36 PDF pages, issued 28 September 2026, supplied by the project owner. SHA-256: a7915e3eea2f89a6a5cb63ac83ce3947aa40abbd79bf42499aa4a17d22174c01. The July draft remains historical data; it is not the new baseline.

Production blockers:
- Verified publication date/reference and effectivity: §41 specifies publication +15 days; issuance is not publication. §40 sets AY 2026–2027 CLOCA/SAA at publication +30 calendar days.
- Category 3 deadlines: §14 rows 1, 2, 6 and 10 mark Category 3 but give deadline text only for Categories 1–2.
- Change report deadline and anchor; immediate notice versus formal report.
- SAA scope/frequency and semester versus annual coverage.
- Annex C identifies a duly authorized affiant in its introductory paragraph but labels the signature as HEAD OF HEI. Confirm delegated-signatory treatment; preparation currently follows the HEI-head signature block without treating account membership as signing authority.
- Routine uploads versus evidence retained/on request.
- PIC ratios are established in §14 row 12 for Categories 1–2 (1:30 with required physical activity, accommodation and/or travel; otherwise 1:50). Confirm APIC counting and interpretation of travel.
- Category 3 eligibility, long-distance/class-size/vulnerable-group definitions.
- Approved acknowledgment templates outside CLOCA/SAA and sequence: Annex N PDF p.36 places acknowledgment before compliance checking; Annex K says reviewed complete. App retains immediate intake receipt and separate signed completeness acknowledgment pending clarification.
- Official working-day calendar and local-suspension treatment.
- Electronic receipt and fallback during outages.
- Authoritative investigation status and activity-specific clearance coverage.
- Annual report exact cutoff, reporting period and format: §38 specifies the last week of August. The app records the window without inventing an exact date.
- Exceptional clearance: §27 requires application 90 days before activity. Confirm the day unit; app records explicitly provisional calendar arithmetic, never permission to proceed.
- Privacy/retention, jurisdiction, hosting, official email and approved signatories.

Further questions:
- Annex M: confirm handling of inapplicable conditional rows in a Yes/No-only matrix, any approved overall-rating methodology, monitoring-personnel/team recording and HEI conforme conventions. The app preserves the 14 template items, human Yes/No responses and explanatory remarks, with an independent operational overall rating and optional printed names/dates. It does not infer a score, signature, acceptance or sanction. Verification examples must be confirmed against evidence actually reviewed.
- Annex L: confirm parent/guardian applicability for adult participants, accepted signing/date conventions and any approved adaptation of the sample privacy notice. Preparation keeps all sample blocks, permits blank particulars for paper completion and does not infer consent from typed names/dates. Personal fields are response-only; completed signed forms remain sensitive evidence retained/uploaded according to confirmed requirements.
- Annex K: confirm whether participating-student totals mean summed activity participations or distinct students, and whether period covered means planned date range or calendar/semester coverage. The app currently uses frozen per-program planned date ranges and summed student counts, excludes other participants, and labels the counting basis in preparation. Confirm approved CHED letterhead, Director IV signatory/delegation rules and applicability to packages outside CLOCA/SAA. A human originals check records signing/notarization inspection; a signed upload flag does not establish notarization.
- Annex J has separate Date/Time columns without prescribing an end-time or time-range field. Preparation currently prints each stop's start date/time in Asia/Manila; descriptions may explain duration/ranges. Confirm any required arrival/departure/end-time convention and whether DOT information may be blank or must show explicit N/A when inapplicable. Recorded references do not verify accreditation.
- Annex I's date of original submission is currently the selected electronic receipt's submitted_at date, or an explicitly HEI-entered external date/reference when no electronic receipt exists. Confirm acceptable external receipt evidence, which original package to reference after multiple corrections, and whether CHED requires supporting submission reference details on the printed form. Immediate notice remains separate from signed RCD filing; the formal “upon conduct” deadline anchor is still unresolved.
- Annex G basic information uses proposed dates but does not say whether venue and participant totals should repeat the plan or show actual implementation. Preparation currently preserves proposed dates from the selected activity revision and prints human-confirmed actual venue/total participants; actual conclusion is separate deadline metadata. Confirm this mapping and any changes to PIC/APIC reporting with CHED.
- Annex F does not specify activity/period coverage or a method for marking inapplicable rows. Confirm certification scope (one calendar/package versus individual activity), allowed N/A notation and applicability-note format. Preparation uses human attestations and explained N/A with separate notes; academic-period selection is organizational only and does not establish legal coverage or CHED acceptance.
- Annex E supplies Low/Medium/High risk levels without a scoring method, acceptance threshold or automatic clearance rule. Confirm any approved rating methodology/sample adaptation; the form records human-entered ratings, contingency measures and contacts without inferring safety or authorization.
- Annex D is a sample with Yes/No answers and three recommendation choices, without a scoring threshold or N/A column. Confirm approved adaptation/applicability and recommendation rules; the preparation form preserves Yes/No, explanatory remarks and a human-selected recommendation without inferring authorization.
- Recurring activities: one ROC per occurrence or consolidated?
- Supplementary/cumulative CLOCA for Categories 2/3?
- Multi-campus/multi-institution representatives?
- Domestic internship/practicum/OJT coverage?
- Incorporated JMC 03/2019, CMO 6/2026 and PSG obligations?
- Category 3 eligibility wording: §12 defines academic fulfillment, while §13 and Annex A also cover well-being/non-curricular activities.
- LGU coordination for research/field study: Annex B permits N/A in specified columns while §14 lists coordination evidence; confirm exceptions.
- Public disclosure of final orders/sanctions?

Resolved by the final order:
- Incident reporting is 48 hours from occurrence (§§14, 17, 25), not the July draft's contradictory 24/72 hours.
- ROC is 72 hours from actual conclusion (§14 row 20).
- SAA is marked for all three categories; pre-departure orientation is marked for Categories 2–3.
- Annex identifiers are B CLOCA, C SAA, D Diagnostic, E RACP, F Certification, G ROC, H Incident, I Change, J Itinerary, K Acknowledgment, L Consent, M Monitoring and N Process.
- One reconsideration is permitted within 15 calendar days of receipt of denial (§27).

Candidate interpretations remain explicit and editable only before confirmation. Copy a policy to propose later changes; never rewrite old snapshots or signed originals. Pending publication does not justify assigning a guessed effectivity date.
